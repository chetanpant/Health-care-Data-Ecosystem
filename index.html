Anjali's data existed. It was in her Bangalore hospital's system. But that system couldn't talk to the Delhi one. Different formats. Different codes. Different rules.

The five standards in her chart

ICDDiagnoses
"What's wrong with the patient?"
A global code for every known disease. Anjali's diabetes = E11.9. Used everywhere — insurance, billing, research.
CPTProcedures
"What was done about it?"
5-digit codes for procedures. Anjali's office visit = 99213. ICD says what's wrong; CPT says what we did.
LOINCLab tests
"What was measured?"
Universal codes for tests and observations. Anjali's HbA1c = 4548-4. Makes her result portable across any hospital.
SNOMED CTClinical terms
"What does the clinician mean?"
350,000+ concepts linked logically. "Fatigue," "MI," "anemia" — all have unique IDs. Makes free text computable.
HL7 & FHIRData exchange
"How do we move the data?"
Other standards say what data means. HL7 and FHIR say how to move it. FHIR is the modern version — used by Apple Health and major EHRs.
